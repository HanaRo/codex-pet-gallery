The two reference projects' licenses cover their own work. Rights for the user-provided reference artwork require separate confirmation. DeepSeek, OpenAI, Codex, and ChatGPT are names of their respective rights holders.

Local source paths, account-specific Pet IDs, temporary links, Library references, and original chat logs are outside this archive.

Scholar Disguise 1.0.0 carries forward DeepSeek-chan's blue hair, ear fins, and whale tail. Codex imagegen produced the white-dragon disguise, animations, and gaze poses. The two drag directions were drawn separately, and the tail-wag palette follows the approved idle.
