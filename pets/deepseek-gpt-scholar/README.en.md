[简体中文](README.md) · **English**

DeepSeek-chan puts on glasses and a white-dragon wig and pretends to study, with a rice bowl hidden behind her book. [Home](../../README.en.md) · [Version gallery](../../VERSIONS.en.md) · [Installation](../../docs/INSTALL.en.md)

## Scholar Disguise 1.0.0

<img src="versions/1.0.0/previews/states/running.gif" width="160" alt="Scholar Disguise lifting her book to sneak a bite">

While you work, she raises the book to hide her mouth. When picked up, she kicks her feet, wags her tail, and reaches up to straighten her slipping wig. The tail-wag palette was adjusted to match idle more closely.
