# v1.0.1 · DeepSeek-chan Foodie Edition

[简体中文](v1.0.1.md) · **English** · [GitHub Release](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/releases/tag/v1.0.1)

She waits with an empty bowl and kicks her feet and wags her tail when picked up. In this edition she holds the same bowl across the idle, tail-wag, and rice-eating animations.

## Downloads

- **[Codex desktop installer ZIP](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/releases/download/v1.0.1/deepseek-chan-1.0.1-pet.zip)** with `pet.json`, the sprite sheet, the layout, and installation instructions.
- **[ChatGPT Work import PNG](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/releases/download/v1.0.1/deepseek-chan-1.0.1-spritesheet.png)** for a v2-compatible custom Pet entry point.

## Changes in this release

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/v1.0.1/pets/deepseek-chan/versions/1.0.1/previews/states/idle.gif" width="128" alt="Foodie Edition waiting with an empty bowl">

- At idle she holds an empty bowl, breathes, and blinks as she waits for rice.
- Dragging her either way lifts her up to kick and wag her tail. She also holds the bowl during the standing tail wag.
- While eating, she raises her chopsticks a little less and keeps the same hair and ear-fin proportions.

[All animations](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/blob/v1.0.1/pets/deepseek-chan/versions/1.0.1/previews/all-states.gif) · [Classic 1.0.0](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

## Installation and compatibility

For desktop Codex, place `pet.json` and `spritesheet.png` in `<CODEX_HOME>/pets/deepseek-chan/` and select the pet in Settings → Pets. For ChatGPT Work, import the PNG through a v2-compatible entry point. [Installation guide](../INSTALL.en.md)
