# v1.1.0 · Introducing Scholar Disguise

[简体中文](v1.1.0.md) · **English** · [GitHub Release](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/releases/tag/v1.1.0)

Scholar Disguise `1.0.0` puts on a white-dragon wig and hides her rice behind a book. As soon as she raises the book, she starts sneaking bites.

## Downloads

- **[Codex desktop installer ZIP](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/releases/download/v1.1.0/deepseek-gpt-scholar-1.0.0-pet.zip)** with `pet.json`, the sprite sheet, and installation instructions.
- **[ChatGPT Work import PNG](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/releases/download/v1.1.0/deepseek-gpt-scholar-1.0.0-spritesheet.png)** for a v2-compatible custom Pet entry point.

## New in this release

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/deepseek-gpt-scholar-v1.0.0/pets/deepseek-gpt-scholar/versions/1.0.0/previews/states/running.gif" width="128" alt="Scholar Disguise lifting her book to sneak a bite">

- While you work, she raises her book to hide her mouth, then lowers it to reveal the rice bowl.
- When picked up, she kicks her feet and wags her tail, then reaches up to straighten her slipping wig.
- She has 16 gaze directions, and the tail-wag frames use the idle palette.

[All animations](https://github.com/HanaRo/codex-pet-gallery/blob/deepseek-gpt-scholar-v1.0.0/pets/deepseek-gpt-scholar/versions/1.0.0/previews/all-states.gif) · [Version gallery](../../VERSIONS.en.md)

## Installation and compatibility

For desktop Codex, place `pet.json` and `spritesheet.png` in `<CODEX_HOME>/pets/deepseek-gpt-scholar/` and select the pet in Settings → Pets. For ChatGPT Work, import the PNG through a v2-compatible entry point. [Installation guide](../INSTALL.en.md)
