# Rights for characters and artwork

[简体中文](ASSET_LICENSE.md) · **English**

The MIT code license applies to newly written tools. Rights for character designs, user-provided references, generated artwork, animation strips, sprite sheets, and previews are recorded separately here.

The original creator and redistribution terms for the user-provided reference artwork remain unconfirmed. Permission for public use, redistribution, adaptation, and commercial use remains to be documented by the maintainer. This record grants no additional rights and makes no claim that the character design is wholly original.

For public distribution, record the confirmed creator, original work, scope of permission, and applicable license text here. Each third-party work remains subject to its own terms. [Credits](CREDITS.en.md)

The remaining details to confirm are:

- The original creator or a verifiable source for the user-provided references.
- The scope of redistribution and adaptation rights.
- The license the maintainer chooses for generated assets they can license.
- Any applicable attribution or noncommercial terms.

Local archiving, hash verification, and reconstruction from frozen frames remain available.
